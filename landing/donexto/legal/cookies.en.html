Donexto Cookie Policy

This policy explains which cookies and local storage Donexto uses on https://www.donexto.com (site) and https://app.donexto.com (web app). It is the same policy in the United States, Mexico, and Canada. Read it together with the Privacy Policy.

This notice is for the United States, Mexico, and Canada. If Donexto is later offered in the European Union, an annex will be published.

Donexto, operated by the Owner, Chihuahua, Mexico. Contact: support@donexto.com.

1. What we use 2. Email provider cookies 3. How to delete them 4. If we ever add analytics

1. What we use

Donexto uses only the session cookies and storage needed for you to sign in and stay in the app. They are technical: without them there is no account or dashboard. They do not require a consent banner.

There is no third-party analytics (gtag / Google Analytics), Facebook pixel, or TikTok pixel on the site or in the app. Social media links on the site are plain links to our profiles; they are not trackers.

On the site, we store in your browser's local storage (localStorage) only the language you chose. It is not a cookie and is not used to track you.

Country and currency: to show prices in your currency, our network provider (Cloudflare) tells us your approximate country based on your IP address. This does not use cookies. If you choose another country or currency, that preference will be saved as technical data in your account or in local storage (coming soon).

2. Email provider cookies

When you connect, the provider for that step (today Microsoft, Google, Yahoo, or Apple) may set its own cookies on its domains. Those cookies belong to them, not to Donexto, and are governed by their policies.

3. How to delete them

You can delete cookies and site data in your browser (Chrome, Edge, Safari, Firefox: Settings → Privacy → cookies and site data). If you delete them, your Donexto session ends and you will need to sign in again.

You can also use a private window; when you close it, the session usually ends.

4. If we ever add analytics

If analytics or marketing cookies are added in the future, they will not turn on by themselves. There will be a banner that is off by default, the same rule in the United States, Mexico, and Canada, and they will only turn on if you explicitly click Accept. Until then, this policy still says: technical only.
