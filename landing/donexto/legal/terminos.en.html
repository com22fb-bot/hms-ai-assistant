2. What the service is

Donexto is an attention layer over the mailbox you connect. It does not replace Gmail or Yahoo. We are not an email provider, a permanent archive of your inbox, or a platform for sending bulk email. The mailbox and messages stay and run on the servers of Google or Yahoo, depending on what you connect.

Donexto classifies incoming email and creates cases instead of emails: you work with cases, not an inbox. We are aware of the importance of family, school, home, work, and the rest of life. We set up alerts intelligently on the information that arrives; you tell us what to alert on and what not.

Donexto sorts each notice into three spheres (Home, Occupation, and Personal) and 13 areas: Money & finance, Shopping & orders, Subscriptions & services, Work & business, Home & family, Health & wellness, Bills & due dates, Travel & bookings, Security & accounts, Government & documents, Education, Agenda, and Social & communications. The app decides the sphere and area on its own and learns from your corrections; it can be wrong, especially at first. Areas are being added in stages.

Prices and currencies: in the Americas, charges are in US dollars (USD); in the European Union and the rest of the world, in euros (EUR), unless the payment processor offers your local currency. Prices and amounts we show converted to your local currency are approximate and for reference. We detect your country at signup to choose currency and availability (coming soon); you can change it, and the country of your card or billing address is final. The service opens country by country in stages.

3. Donexto account and email providers

Outlook, Hotmail, Microsoft 365, Gmail, Yahoo, and iCloud are read in read-only mode. Outlook/Hotmail: you sign in at Microsoft (Mail.Read); Donexto does not ask for the mailbox password. Gmail: Google shows that it has not yet verified the app; tap Advanced and then Go to Donexto. This is early access (Google limits unverified apps to 100 users). The permission is gmail.readonly and can be revoked at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Yahoo and iCloud: app password (not the account password), read-only; Yahoo is still pending official authorization (mail-r). The Yahoo or iCloud app password is requested only in the app (app.donexto.com), not on this site. With Gmail, Yahoo, and iCloud you also receive the Donexto verification email.

Available today

  • Yahoo: direct IMAP connection with an app password, while Yahoo has not granted official authorization (mail-r). Read-only and revocable in your Yahoo account security (login.yahoo.com/account/security).

5. How we process email

Processing is automatic: we classify incoming email to show what needs attention. Part of that classification happens on our servers (Railway). When model analysis is active, a processor (today OpenAI) may receive message fragments only for that classification. You decide what is alerted and what is not. Donexto shows that information in your dashboard; it does not describe every possible system failure.

5a. Account retention and deletion

If your free trial ends and you do not subscribe, we keep your account and its data for 1 more week. If your subscription is not renewed, we keep your account for 1 month after it lapses. After that period, an automatic maintenance process that runs every day at 3:00 a.m. (Mexico City time) deletes the account, the connected mailboxes, and their data in Donexto. Your email stays intact at your provider. We email you twice before deletion: at the midpoint of the period and 1 day before.
