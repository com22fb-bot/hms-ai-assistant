1. What data we use and why

Today you can connect these mailboxes:

  • Outlook / Hotmail / Microsoft 365: you sign in at Microsoft (OAuth, Mail.Read permission). Read-only and revocable in your Microsoft account. Donexto does not ask for the mailbox password.
  • Gmail (and Google Workspace, if your organization allows it): reading with the gmail.readonly permission. Google may warn that it has not verified the app; access is early (up to 100 users) and can be revoked at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
  • Yahoo: direct IMAP connection (imap.mail.yahoo.com, port 993, SSL) with an app password you generate at login.yahoo.com/account/security (External connections), pending Yahoo's official authorization (mail-r). Read-only (EXAMINE and BODY.PEEK) and revocable at Yahoo. The app password is stored encrypted and requested only in the app (app.donexto.com), not on this site.
  • Apple iCloud Mail (@icloud.com, @me.com, @mac.com): you connect with an app-specific password. Donexto reads only that email, read-only (imap.mail.me.com, port 993 with SSL, EXAMINE and BODY.PEEK). It does not mark as read, move, or delete. The app password is stored encrypted. You can disconnect the mailbox in Donexto (it is deleted) and revoke the password at account.apple.com anytime. It is also requested only in the app.

Coming soon (not yet available): AOL. We do not claim it already connects.

With Gmail, Yahoo, and iCloud we send you a Donexto verification email; without that step there is no access. Outlook and Hotmail are identified at Microsoft. Gmail uses the unverified app notice. Yahoo uses an app password while official authorization (mail-r) is pending. None of them marks your emails as read.

Depending on the mailbox you connect, the system accesses, as needed to build your attention dashboard:

  • Account data: your email address and profile name to identify you as a user.
  • Read authorization: for Outlook/Hotmail, Microsoft tokens (Mail.Read) stored encrypted. For Gmail, Google tokens stored encrypted. For Yahoo, the app password (or later, the OAuth token if Yahoo authorizes mail-r) stored encrypted. For iCloud, the app-specific password stored encrypted.
  • Email metadata and fragments: sender, subject, date and, when needed to classify, the message text.
  • Email classification: the life sphere (Home, Occupation, or Personal), the area (Money & finance, Shopping & orders, Subscriptions & services, Work & business, Home & family, Health & wellness, Bills & due dates, Travel & bookings, Security & accounts, Government & documents, Education, Agenda, and Social & communications), the priority, and the data the notice contains, such as amounts, merchants, due dates, and order, tracking, invoice, or booking numbers.
  • Country and currency: the approximate country you connect from, inferred from your IP address by our network provider (Cloudflare), to show you prices and amounts in your currency and to know whether the service is available in your country. You can change it. If you subscribe, the country of your card or billing address is what counts. (Detection at signup will be enabled soon.)
  • Donexto usage data: your settings, your corrections, and essential technical logs (session, error, and security logs).

Purpose: to classify incoming email and create cases (cases, not emails) in your dashboard. You choose what is alerted and what is not.

Sensitive data: some emails contain health information (appointments, results, prescriptions) or financial information (charges, deposits, statements, debts). By connecting your mailbox and accepting this notice, you give us your express consent to process that information only to classify it and show it in your dashboard. Health notifications on your phone show no details, and verification codes never appear in a notification: only inside the app. You can withdraw your consent by disconnecting the mailbox or deleting your account.

Converted amounts: “money detected” is what appears in your emails, not your bank balance. When we show an amount or price in your local currency, the conversion is approximate and for reference only; the charge is made in the currency shown at checkout.

We do not use that data for secondary purposes. We do not sell it or “share” it for advertising. We do not use it to train public or general-purpose artificial intelligence models.

You must have the right to connect that mailbox. If it is work email, your organization's policies may prohibit it. Donexto is not your employer's authorization.

Your full mailbox stays with the provider (Microsoft, Google, Yahoo, or Apple). Donexto is not an archive of your inbox. What we do keep—and need to keep so the dashboard exists the next day—is the minimum for the attention layer: the account, the encrypted read authorization, metadata, classifications, corrections, and technical logs. That information stays in our database after you close the browser, until you disconnect the mailbox or delete the account.

In principle, we request and keep only what is needed to provide that attention layer. Apart from the account retention periods in section 7a, we do not publish fixed retention periods by data type. When you leave, we block or delete as required by applicable law, as described below.

2. How we use mailbox data

For the mailboxes available today (Outlook, Hotmail, Microsoft 365, Gmail, Yahoo, and iCloud), Donexto commits to the following:

  • We do not use your mailbox content for ads, ours or third parties'.
  • We do not sell your inbox, your contacts, or the content of your emails.
  • We do not use your email to train public or general-purpose artificial intelligence models.
  • We use it only to provide the attention layer described in this policy, and to operate it with technical processors.
  • Donexto staff do not read your email as an ordinary practice.

Google (Gmail and Google Workspace)

Use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements: https://developers.google.com/terms/api-services-user-data-policy. You can revoke Donexto's access at https://myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Yahoo

Yahoo connects today via secure IMAP (imap.mail.yahoo.com, port 993 with SSL) with an app password you generate. It is not your Yahoo account password. We store it encrypted and only read (EXAMINE and BODY.PEEK). The Donexto verification email is still required. OAuth access is kept for when Yahoo authorizes mail-r. To stop access: disconnect the mailbox in Donexto (the app password is deleted) and revoke it in your Yahoo account security.

AOL is not available yet.

Apple iCloud Mail

iCloud connects with an app-specific password (two-factor authentication on the Apple account is required). Donexto reads the mailbox via secure IMAP, read-only. We store that password encrypted to keep reading while the connection is active. To stop access: disconnect the mailbox in Donexto and revoke the password at account.apple.com → Sign-In and Security → App-Specific Passwords.

Microsoft (Outlook, Hotmail, Microsoft 365)

Outlook, Hotmail, and Microsoft 365 connect today by signing in at Microsoft (OAuth). Donexto requests the Mail.Read permission (read-only) and does not ask for the mailbox password. Tokens are stored encrypted. To stop access: disconnect the mailbox in Donexto and revoke Donexto at account.microsoft.com/privacy/app-access (or your Microsoft account's app permissions page).

We do not support Proton, Tuta, or any end-to-end encryption provider that prevents authorized external reading.

3. Shared responsibility

Donexto encrypts the read authorization (Microsoft tokens, Google tokens, the Yahoo or iCloud app password, and later a Yahoo OAuth token if mail-r arrives). That alone does not close the permission on the mailbox side.

If you stop using Donexto: disconnect the mailbox or delete the account in the app. For Outlook/Hotmail, also revoke Donexto in your Microsoft account. For Gmail, also revoke Donexto at https://myaccount.google.com/permissions. For Yahoo, disconnect the mailbox in Donexto and revoke the app password at login.yahoo.com/account/security.

4. Processors

To run the attention layer, Donexto uses these processors:

  • Microsoft, if you connect Outlook, Hotmail, or Microsoft 365: identity and mailbox reading via Microsoft Graph (Mail.Read).
  • Google, if you connect Gmail: identity (when you sign in with Google) and mailbox reading via the Gmail API.
  • Yahoo, if you connect Yahoo: mailbox reading via IMAP with the app password (not the account password). Donexto sends it only to imap.mail.yahoo.com to open the mailbox read-only, and stores it encrypted.
  • Supabase, Railway, Cloudflare, and Resend: Donexto account data, API, hosting of the site and the app, and transactional email (Donexto verification and notices).
  • OpenAI, when model analysis is active: it may receive message fragments only to classify what needs attention. We do not use your email to train OpenAI's public models.

GitHub is not a processor for this service.

That transfer takes place under the data processing agreement (DPA) and, where the provider itself offers them, standard contractual clauses (SCC) or another equivalent mechanism of that provider. The Owner does not state that it has adopted its own standard clauses or a custom contract with each cloud.

If the law of your location requires informed consent for that transfer, this section is that notice: we use the cloud in the United States to provide the service. The signup box only asks you to accept the Terms and this Policy; the transfer details are here, not in that box.

If a breach compromises your Donexto data, we will notify you by email within the time limits set by law. The official channel to report it or ask about it is support@donexto.com.

6. Your rights

You can see your user data and correct classifications in the app, or ask us by email. You can disconnect the mailbox or delete the account in Donexto. In Gmail, you can also revoke Donexto at Google. In Yahoo, you can revoke Donexto in your Yahoo account's connected apps. You can request an export of the data generated within Donexto (account settings and classification history). That does not include exporting your full inbox: it lives and stays with the email provider.

To exercise these rights: support@donexto.com, or Settings → Delete account if you want to leave.

7. Leaving, blocking, and deletion

You can leave at any time:

  • From the app: Settings → Delete account.
  • By email: support@donexto.com.

When we process your request: we stop reading your mailbox; we delete or stop using the read authorization in Donexto (Microsoft tokens, Google tokens, the Yahoo or iCloud app password, or Yahoo OAuth tokens if they arrive); and we block or delete Donexto data as required by applicable law. In Mexico, the prior blocking required by the LFPDPPP when applicable. Infrastructure backups may keep copies for a while, until they rotate; those copies are not used to classify your mailbox again.

Disconnecting the mailbox is not the same as deleting the Donexto account. The first stops reading that email; the second closes your user and the associated Donexto data.

In Gmail, to also close the permission at Google: https://myaccount.google.com/permissions. In Outlook/Hotmail, revoke Donexto in your Microsoft account. In Yahoo, disconnect the mailbox in Donexto and revoke the app password in your Yahoo account security. In iCloud, revoke the app-specific password at account.apple.com.

7a. How long we keep your account

Free trial without a subscription: we keep the account and its data for 1 week after the trial ends. Subscription not renewed: 1 month after it lapses. After that, an automatic daily process (3:00 a.m., Mexico City time) deletes the account, connected mailboxes, read authorizations, classifications, and cases. Before deletion we send you two email reminders: at the midpoint of the period and 1 day before. When you connect a mailbox, we import the last 90 days of email with the monthly plan and the last 6 months with the annual plan.
